2dim
Two Dimensional Shapes

2D shapes is shapes that only have length and width, without depth or volume

These shapes can only be seen from one side and are usually drawn on a flat surface like paper or a screen.

3dim
Three Dimensional Shapes

3D shapes have length, width, and depth, giving them volume.

These shapes can be viewed from multiple angles and exist in physical space, like cubes, spheres, and pyramids.
